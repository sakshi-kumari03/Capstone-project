# 🚗 Used Car Sales EDA

## 📌 Overview
This project performs an in-depth **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** on a large dataset of used car sales (122,144 records) across the United States from 2019–2020.

The goal is to uncover patterns in vehicle pricing, understand key factors affecting resale value, and provide data-driven insights for buyers, sellers, and dealerships.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement
The used car market is highly fragmented, with prices varying significantly based on multiple factors like mileage, age, brand, and engine type.

This project aims to:
- Identify key factors influencing car prices
- Analyze market demand and trends
- Help stakeholders make informed pricing decisions

---

## 🧠 Business Objectives
- Analyze relationships between vehicle attributes and price
- Understand demand by make, body type, and drive type
- Detect anomalies in the dataset
- Provide actionable insights for pricing strategy

---

## 📊 Dataset Information
- **Records:** 122,144 vehicles  
- **Time Period:** 2019–2020  
- **Features include:**
  - Make & Model
  - Year of manufacture
  - Mileage
  - Engine type
  - Body type
  - Drive type
  - Number of cylinders
  - Selling price  

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
- **Language:** Python  
- **Libraries:**
  - Pandas
  - NumPy
  - Matplotlib
  - Seaborn  

---

## 🔍 Key Analysis Performed
- Price distribution analysis  
- Mileage vs price relationship  
- Impact of vehicle age on price  
- Popular car brands and body types  
- Drive type & cylinder influence  
- Year-wise sales trends  
- Correlation heatmap of numerical features  

---

## 📈 Insights
- Vehicle age and mileage strongly impact resale value  
- Certain brands consistently retain higher value  
- SUVs and sedans dominate market demand  
- Higher cylinders often correlate with higher prices  
- Clear negative correlation between mileage and price
